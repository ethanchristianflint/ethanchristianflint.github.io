I registered on the Department of Homeland Security's accessibility training portal and started the Trusted Tester for Web training, six modules ending in a certification exam.

Trusted Tester is a government-issued certification in testing websites for Section 508 conformance, using a repeatable test process so results are consistent from tester to tester. It's free, and it doesn't mean working for DHS.

## The plan

- Work through the six modules. Modules 1 and 2 (what Section 508 is, and the Section 508 standards for web) are done as of today.
- Pass the practice exam with at least 90% before attempting the certification exam.
- Take the certification exam in December 2026.
- Write a short entry here after each module: what it covered and what surprised me.

## Why start here

The deadlines for state and local government websites under ADA Title II begin in April 2027. Cities and counties need people who can test their sites and explain what to fix. Trusted Tester is the most direct, no-cost way to learn a recognized testing method.
